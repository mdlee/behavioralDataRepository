# Lee, Blanco, & Bo (2016) take-the-best environments

Lee, M. D., Blanco, G., & Bo, N. (2016). Testing take-the-best in new and changing environments. *Behavior Research Methods*, 49, 1420–1431. [osf.io/yd7mw](https://osf.io/yd7mw/)

Cue-criterion environments for applying take-the-best, weighted additive, and tally. **This deposit is environment structure, not human choices.** Four new domains (bridges, hamburgers, theme parks, US universities) and four city domains that change across years (US, UK, Italy, Germany).

## Files

- `newEnvironments.csv` — 1,715 item–cue rows
- `changingEnvironments.csv` — 9,378 item–cue–year rows

## Columns

- `environment`, `item`, `criterion`, `cue`, `cueValue` (`0`/`1`)
- changing environments also have `year`
