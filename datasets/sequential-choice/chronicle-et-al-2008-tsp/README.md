# Chronicle et al. (2008) traveling salesperson problems

Chronicle, E. P., MacGregor, J. N., Lee, M. D., Ormerod, T. C., & Hughes, P. (2008). Individual differences in optimization problem solving: Reconciling conflicting results. *Journal of Problem Solving*, 2, 41–49.

University of Hawaii pencil-and-paper tours. The paper reports 112 students; this file is the 82 who produced valid tours for all 9 thirty-point and all 9 forty-point problems.

## Files

- `coordinates.csv` — point locations
- `tours.csv` — visit order (`point` at each `step`)
