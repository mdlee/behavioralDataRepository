# Lee & Lee (2017) crowd majority

Lee, M. D., & Lee, M. N. (2017). The relationship between crowd majority and accuracy for binary decisions. *Judgment and Decision Making*, 12, 328–343. [osf.io/3t3fp](https://osf.io/3t3fp/)

**Problem-level** majority counts, not person-level trials. Each row is one binary question: how many people in the crowd chose option 1 (`majority`), crowd size (`crowd`), and whether that majority was correct (`accuracy`).

## Files

`aflGames.csv`, `nflGames.csv`, `fantasyFootball.csv`, `marchMadness.csv`, `triviaQuestions.csv`, `cancerDiagnosis.csv`, `durationPerception.csv`, `esp.csv`, `roulette.csv` — **7,573** questions in total.
