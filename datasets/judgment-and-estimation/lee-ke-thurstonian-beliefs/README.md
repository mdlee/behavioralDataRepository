# Lee & Ke Thurstonian belief rankings

Lee, M. D., & Ke, M. Y. (in press). Modeling individual differences in beliefs and opinions using Thurstonian models. In J. Musolino, P. Hemmer, & J. Sommer (Eds.), *The Science of Beliefs*. Cambridge University Press. [osf.io/sbgkw](https://osf.io/sbgkw/)

Survey rankings. Location, age, gender, and timestamps from the original survey files are not included. **`participant` IDs restart in each domain.**

| Domain | People | Item–rank rows |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| NBA players | 2,810 | 98,976 |
| US presidents (past 50 years) | 145 | 921 |
| Months of the year | 21 | 219 |
| Worst places to live in the US | 161 | 742 |

## Files

Each domain has a ranking file (`nbaPlayers.csv`, …) and an `*Items.csv` name list.

## Columns

- `item`, `name`
- `rank` — 1 is first in that person’s list; people need not rank every item
