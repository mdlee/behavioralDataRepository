# unpublished ranking

Partial rankings collected as a course assignment at UC Irvine (IRB 4286). Each row is one item the person placed. Unranked items are omitted. `rank` is 1 for the item they put first.

`participant` IDs are anonymous integers and match `bart`, `gasPrices`, and `fishPrices` for the same person.

| Quarter | Format |
|---------|--------|
| W2023 | Comma-separated orders (cities, countries, states, rivers, presidents, and related sets) |
| W2024 | Comma-separated orders |
| F2025 | Eight drag-and-drop sets (cities, holidays, events, movies, halftime shows, rivers, and two further sets) |

`truthRank` is filled when the item belongs to a set whose correct order is known (1 = largest, earliest, or westmost, depending on the set). It is blank otherwise.

W2024 and F2025 drop people who declined research consent. W2023 has no separate consent item; finished responses are kept.

## Citation

Unpublished course dataset. A published ranking wisdom-of-crowds study with different people is Lee, Steyvers, & Miller (2014), already in `lee-steyvers-miller-2014-rankings`.
