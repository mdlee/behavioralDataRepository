# Lee, Zhang, & Shi (2011) Price is Right bids

Lee, M. D., Zhang, S., & Shi, J. (2011). The wisdom of the crowd playing the Price is Right. *Memory & Cognition*, 39, 914–923.

`bids.csv` columns: `episode`, `round`, player IDs `P1`–`P4`, bids `E1`–`E4`, `truth`. `episode` is filled on every row. When every contestant overbid, `truth` is `allOverbid`.
