# Lee & Shi (2010) small-group wisdom of the crowd

Lee, M. D., & Shi, J. (2010). The accuracy of small-group estimation and the wisdom of crowds. In R. Catrambone & S. Ohlsson (Eds.), *Proceedings of the 32nd Annual Conference of the Cognitive Science Society* (pp. 1124–1129).

Price estimates for visual product stimuli in individual, primed, cooperative, and competitive (“Price is Right”) groups.

`estimates.csv` is long-form. Product names are filled in where the source mapped image numbers. One competitive group is missing a trial in the original file (skipped when empty).
